ABOUT ME

Researching materials for the future of energy

My research is driven by current challenges facing modern energy and infrastructure systems. I focus on understanding materials performance and degradation under realistic operating conditions, with the goal of producing research that extends beyond the laboratory and addresses problems relevant to industry.

Through experimentally driven research, I aim to develop practical insights and solutions that improve materials reliability, infrastructure integrity, safety, and performance across conventional and emerging energy technologies.
