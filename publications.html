Additive Manufacturing of Glass Fiber-Reinforced Polypropylene and Its Behavior Under Controlled Hydrogen Exposure

A. Qureshi, Ulises Martin, Abdul Mannan, and Homero Castaneda

RESEARCH SUMMARY

This study investigated the mechanical performance of additively manufactured glass fiber-reinforced polypropylene following hydrogen exposure at 35 and 70 bar total pressure for 3 and 5 days. Tensile testing showed that the ultimate tensile strength decreased from 10.72 MPa in the as-printed condition to 7.31 MPa following 3 days of exposure at 7 bar hydrogen partial pressure, representing a 31.8% reduction. The findings demonstrated that elevated hydrogen exposure conditions could significantly affect the mechanical integrity of additively manufactured polymer composites, highlighting the importance of material qualification for hydrogen infrastructure applications.

Advances in Photothermal Catalysts for Solar-Driven Hydrogen Production

A. Qureshi, M. A. Wahab, A. Abdel-Wahab, H. Castaneda, and A. Abdala

This review examined advances in photothermal catalysts for solar-driven hydrogen production, focusing on the influence of catalyst composition, light absorption, and photothermal conversion mechanisms. It evaluated oxide-, selenide-, and telluride-based catalytic systems and discussed their synthesis approaches, hydrogen-production performance, and underlying reaction mechanisms. The review identified challenges associated with catalytic efficiency, long-term stability, and scalability, highlighting opportunities for developing more effective solar-to-hydrogen conversion technologies.

Advancing Steel Protection with Ceria@Talc-8-Hydroxyquinoline Modified Polyurethane Coatings

A. Qureshi, S. Habib, Muhammad Faisal, Ramazan Kahraman, Elsadig Mahdi Ahmed, and R. A. Shakoor

This study developed multifunctional polyurethane coatings incorporating ceria-modified talc nanocarriers loaded with 8-hydroxyquinoline to improve corrosion protection of steel. Structural characterization and electrochemical testing demonstrated enhanced coating barrier properties and corrosion resistance, achieving approximately 99.9% corrosion inhibition efficiency after four weeks of immersion. The findings demonstrated the effectiveness of combining functionalized nanocarriers with corrosion inhibitors to develop durable protective coatings for steel infrastructure.

TiO₂-Mesoporous Ceria Carrier Modified with Sodium Benzoate: An Innovative Polyurethane Matrix for Enhanced Corrosion Protection of Steel

S. Habib, A. Qureshi, Samra Sajjad, Elsadig Mahdi Ahmed, and R. A. Shakoor

This study developed polyurethane coatings reinforced with TiO₂/mesoporous CeO₂ hybrid nanocarriers loaded with sodium benzoate to improve corrosion protection of steel. Electrochemical testing in 3.5 wt.% NaCl solution demonstrated an impedance of approximately 96.65 GΩ·cm² after four weeks of immersion, nearly four orders of magnitude higher than the unmodified polyurethane coating. The results demonstrated that combining enhanced barrier properties with inhibitor-based protection substantially improved coating durability and corrosion resistance.

Modified Halloysite Nanotubes Decorated with Ceria for Synergistic Corrosion Inhibition of Polyolefin Based Smart Composite Coatings

A. Qureshi, S. Habib, Mudassir Nawaz, R. A. Shakoor, Ramazan Kahraman, and Elsadig Mahdi Ahmed

This study developed smart polyolefin coatings incorporating ceria-decorated halloysite nanotubes modified with sodium dodecyl sulfate (SDS) to provide active corrosion inhibition. Electrochemical and materials characterization demonstrated improved corrosion resistance through the combined action of protective cerium-based compounds and SDS-derived inhibition at cathodic and anodic regions of the steel surface. The results highlighted the effectiveness of multifunctional nanocarriers for improving the protective performance of polymeric coatings in aggressive environments.

Corrosion Inhibition Performance of Polyolefin Smart Self-Healing Composite Coatings Modified with ZnO@β-Cyclodextrin Hybrid Particles

S. Habib, A. Qureshi, R. A. Shakoor, Ramazan Kahraman, Noora Hamad Al-Qahtani, and Elsadig Mahdi Ahmed

This study developed smart polyolefin coatings incorporating ZnO@β-cyclodextrin hybrid particles loaded with 2-mercaptobenzothiazole to provide active corrosion protection. Electrochemical testing demonstrated improved coating resistance, with impedance increasing from approximately 86.7 to 98.7 GΩ·cm² after 20 days of immersion, attributed to the synergistic inhibition effects of ZnO and the encapsulated corrosion inhibitor. The findings highlighted the potential of functionalized hybrid nanocarriers for developing durable, self-healing protective coatings.
